# VoltSeal

Professional website for VoltSeal, a smart Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) solutions company focused on helping commercial and industrial businesses reduce energy costs, improve renewable energy utilisation, and lower their carbon footprint.

## Features

- Responsive and modern UI
- Energy storage solutions showcase
- Time-shifting / Energy Arbitrage
- Peak Shaving
- Diesel Generator (DG) Avoidance
- Green Charging Solutions
- Solar + BESS comparison
- Benefits and energy savings information
- Company vision and mission
- Leadership section
- Contact section

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- PHP

- Home
- Solutions

- Leadership

## Purpose

The website presents VoltSeal's BESS solutions and communicates how battery storage can help commercial and industrial facilities reduce energy costs, improve operational resilience, and support sustainability goals.
